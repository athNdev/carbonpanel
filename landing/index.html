MORE WORLDS. ROOM TO BREATHE.

Let quiet worlds rest.

Keep your survival world, creative sandbox and next modpack adventure ready to wake whenever you want to play. Deep sleep frees RAM between sessions, so several servers can share your personal machine or a server with limited memory.

Give RAM to the worlds you’re playing, and leave room for your other apps. Deep sleep stops the JVM; freeze pauses CPU work but keeps RAM allocated. Joining through the configured proxy wakes a sleeping world. Allow time for the server to start.

BEFORE YOU JOIN

A few good
questions.

What do I need to run it?+

You provide a compatible Docker host, enough resources for your Minecraft servers, and persistent storage. The Docker Compose guide walks through the setup.

Can I run modded servers?+

Yes. Carbon Panel supports server software including Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, alongside Vanilla and Paper. Use Modpack Studio to assemble and share a pack. Check compatibility for your chosen loader and Minecraft version.

Where do my worlds and data live?+

On the storage you configure for your own installation. The panel database and server worlds are persistent data. Plan your storage and backups before creating a server.

How does waking a server work?+

Supported sleep modes let the proxy respond to connections and trigger a wake. Cold starts take time and may require a player to retry. Freeze retains memory; deep sleep releases JVM memory. See the sleep and wake guide for conditions.

Is Carbon Panel really free?+

The OSS panel is free and open source under MPL-2.0. You supply and pay for your infrastructure. You do not need a Carbon Cloud account to self-host.
