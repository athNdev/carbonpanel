# Homepage artwork

The original terrain artwork is `landing/assets/graphite-terrain.webp`.
It was created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 12 November 2026, then
encoded as a WebP for the website. It is a decorative material study, not an
image of a real Minecraft world or product interface. Approved brand assets
remain separate and unmodified.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original website hero artwork for Carbon Panel, a professional open-source Minecraft server management product. Create one exquisite, tactile studio photograph-like 3D render of a miniature stepped landscape carved from dark graphite and basalt. Broad square terraces, a few precise cubic cuts, fine mineral grain, a tiny sheltered opening with an almost imperceptible warm light. Minecraft is suggested by the block-based terrain, with architectural model craftsmanship. Restrained charcoal and warm silver palette, deep neutral near-black background (#141414), soft directional light from upper left, carefully rendered contact shadows. Elevated three-quarter view, landscape composition 3:2. The main terrain sits on the right two thirds and low in frame, generous empty dark space upper left, isolated edges fading into the dark. Visually compelling material study with sophisticated negative space. No text, no logos, no interface, no floating cubes, no neon, no grass textures, no characters, no game items, no cartoon style. This is a standalone art asset, not a website screenshot.

The marketing fonts are IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, Latin WOFF2 subsets
from Google's font distribution. Their SIL Open Font Licences are in
`landing/assets/fonts/`. Panel fragments use the existing vendored Inter and
JetBrains Mono files with their original attribution.

## Crafted world revision

The homepage uses `landing/assets/graphite-world.webp`, generated with the
built-in ImageGen tool on 12 November 2026 using the original terrain as an edit
reference. The WebP retains the generated alpha channel. The same original
artwork supplies both the hero and the closer crop near the closing action.
The small doorway light and light on its threshold are decorative CSS layers,
confined to those surfaces, that follow the sample server's completed state.
The piece depicts an imagined miniature, not a real hosted world.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit / stylized-concept. Asset type: original premium website hero artwork for Carbon Panel, self-hosted Minecraft server software. Input image: edit target and material reference. Develop this stepped graphite/basalt miniature landscape into a lovingly crafted inhabitable place. Preserve the dark natural rough stone, cubical terraced geometry, photographic quality and elevated three-quarter camera. Give it a memorable isolated silhouette, with the high ridge on the right and a winding deliberate stair path rising from the lower left foreground to a small sheltered rectangular doorway on the right-middle. Add a tiny refined dark timber lintel/threshold and one very small wooden crossing over a narrow cut in the stone: subtle traces of someone making a home, not a city or castle. Doorway interior should be dark/unlit so a warm lamp can be added interactively in code. Strong natural raking studio light catching the top planes, legible surface details, deep charcoal sides, quiet premium architectural miniature. Show the entire object with margin all around, landscape 3:2 canvas. The terrain should fill most of the canvas, no big blank upper-left field. Background completely transparent with real alpha. No ground plane beyond the terrain, no cast shadow cloud or halo outside the object, no fog, no ambient gray haze, no background gradient, no bloom, no text or logos, no people, no scattered floating cubes, no neon, no voxel game textures. Keep the Minecraft reference in the intentional block geometry and tiny constructed detail.
